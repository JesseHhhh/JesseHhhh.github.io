What is SEO?

Search Engine Optimization is the enhancement and alteration of a website's content in order for it to show up more often in search results.

Why is SEO Important?

SEO keeps one's webpage well-organized trustworthy which provides the ability for webpages that are most relevant to a search to be the top result whenever searching for terms related to the website.

Where is SEO Used?

It is often found on most websites, especially business sites, stores, and news sites.
