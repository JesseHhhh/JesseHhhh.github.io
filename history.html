History of SEO

SEO was applied and used before the term was even created. Around the mid 1990s people added keywords on their webpages to raise their popularity.

Evolution of SEO

Around the launch of Google, links became an important aspect in SEO because if a website's link was added onto other websites (Backlinks), it raises their position and relevancy in search results.
